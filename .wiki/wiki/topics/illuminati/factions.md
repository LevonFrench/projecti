# Illuminati Factions

In the classic game of **Illuminati**, players choose one of the core secret societies (Illuminati cards) to act as the heart of their global conspiracy. Each faction has unique stats, passive abilities, and specialized win conditions (victory goals).

Below is the deep mechanical breakdown of each faction and our proposed design concepts for translating them into a modern card game layout.

---

## 1. Bavarian Illuminati
*The traditional, power-hungry conspirators.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 10
*   **Income**: 9 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Privileged Attack* — Once per turn, the Bavarians can pay 5 Megabucks to declare an attack "Privileged". No other player can assist or interfere with a privileged attack (neither for the attacker nor the defender).
*   **Victory Goal**: Control groups with a total combined Power of 35 or more (including their own 10 Power).

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Dominator (Power-focused Control)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Privileged Action*
    *   Once per round, the player can pay a high resource cost to make an action card play "Privileged", preventing the opponent from playing any counters, responses, or interrupts.
*   **Strategic Focus**: High-power, raw-statistic card chains that establish dominance through direct, un-counterable plays.

---

## 2. Bermuda Triangle
*The enigmatic, reality-warping masters of coordination.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 8
*   **Income**: 9 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Spatial Reorganization* — At the end of their turn, the Bermuda player can freely reorganize their controlled groups, changing which groups are connected to which arrows (provided alignments/arrows match), without making any rolls.
*   **Victory Goal**: Control at least one group of each of the 10 alignments (or at least 3 groups of each of any 5 alignments, depending on the ruleset).

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Tactician (Layout & Connection Manipulation)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Network Re-Link*
    *   At the end of a turn, the player can swap the positions of nodes or change linkages within their card network without expending standard action points.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Adaptive grid and layout control, rapidly shifting assets to meet defensive needs or build synergy chains.

---

## 3. Discordian Society
*The chaotic, parody-religious agents of discord.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 8
*   **Income**: 8 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Chaos Mastery* — +4 bonus on all attempts to control *Weird* groups. The Discordians are completely immune to any attacks from *Government* or *Straight* groups.
*   **Victory Goal**: Control 5 *Weird* groups.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Anarchist (Rule Bending & Chaos Engines)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Rule Anarchy*
    *   The player is immune to alignment-specific restrictions (e.g., they can play Weird cards adjacent to Straight cards or bypass requirements that dictate sequential card plays).
*   **Strategic Focus**: Breaking structural gameplay rules, utilizing erratic card combinations, and countering highly organized structural decks.

---

## 4. Gnomes of Zurich
*The international banking elite.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 7
*   **Income**: 12 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Slush Fund* — At the end of their turn, the Gnomes can move Megabucks freely between any treasuries in their power structure.
*   **Victory Goal**: Accumulate a combined total of 150 Megabucks across their power structure treasuries.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Banker (Resource Buffering & Wealth Accumulation)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Slush Fund*
    *   Allows the player to pool or store unused energy and resources directly onto individual card nodes. These resources can be transferred dynamically between nodes at the end of a turn to fund massive late-game plays.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Heavy economy building, resource preservation, and explosive, high-cost combos funded by banked wealth.

---

## 5. The Network
*The information brokers and data miners.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 7
*   **Income**: 9 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Information Overload* — The Network draws and turns over two cards at the beginning of the draw phase, instead of the standard one.
*   **Victory Goal**: Control groups with a total combined Transferable Power of 25 or more.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Intel Broker (Card Advantage & Hand Disruption)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Information Mining*
    *   The player maintains a larger maximum hand size and can pay resources to force opponents to reveal their hand, gaining vital tactical information.
*   **Strategic Focus**: High card draw, extensive card selection options, and preemptive hand disruption strategies.

---

## 6. Servants of Cthulhu
*The fanatical cultists seeking the end of the world.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 9
*   **Income**: 7 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Sacrificial Destruction* — +2 bonus on any attempt to destroy any group card.
*   **Victory Goal**: Successfully destroy 8 groups.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Zealot (Destruction & Graveyard Interaction)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Sacrificial Harvest*
    *   Grants bonuses and resource refunds whenever a card (either the player's own or the opponent's) is destroyed. Reduces the action/resource cost needed to destroy target cards.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Board clearing, aggressive removal strategies, and deriving power from sending cards to the discard pile.

---

## 7. Society of Assassins
*The silent killers operating in the shadows.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 8
*   **Income**: 8 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Quiet Takeover* — +4 bonus on any attempt to neutralize a group.
*   **Victory Goal**: Control 6 *Violent* groups.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Assassin (Precision Neutralization & Silencing)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Precision Lockout*
    *   Whenever the player successfully neutralizes or blocks an opponent's card action, they apply a "Silenced" status to that node, disabling its passive abilities or card text for the subsequent turn.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Direct targeting, locking down key opponent abilities, and precision control.

---

## 8. UFOs
*The mysterious aliens with hidden agendas.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 6
*   **Income**: 8 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Multiple Operations* — The UFOs can make or assist in two attacks per turn, whereas other groups are limited to one.
*   **Victory Goal**: Secret Goal — The UFO player selects one of the other players' victory conditions in secret at the start of the game.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Alien (Double Plays & Shape-shifting)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Double Deploy*
    *   The player can execute two card plays or minor actions per turn where other factions are restricted to one, allowing rapid board development.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Action economy, high tempo, playing multiple low-cost cards, and shifting victory conditions mid-game.

---

## 9. Church of the SubGenius
*The slack-seeking, tax-exempt satirical cult.*

### Classic Statistics & Rules
*   **Power**: 6
*   **Income**: 9 MB
*   **Special Ability**: *Slack Reclamation* — Whenever any of the Church's controlled groups are captured, neutralized, or destroyed, the Church receives a cash payout of 10 Megabucks from the bank.
*   **Victory Goal**: Accumulate 100 Megabucks or control a set number of groups.

### Modern Adaptation Design Concept
*   **Playstyle**: **The Slacker (Punish & Slack Payoff)**
*   **Mechanic**: *Slack Compensation*
    *   Whenever one of the player's controlled cards is destroyed or stolen, they immediately draw cards and gain a temporary pool of action points, turning losses into immediate counter-play opportunities.
*   **Strategic Focus**: Punish strategies, resource recovery, and high-risk setups that benefit from opponent aggression.
