# Group Cards Database

In **Illuminati**, players expand their puppet networks by controlling **Group cards**. Each group represents a social, political, or economic entity with specific attributes:
*   **Power (P)**: The card's default strength used to control, neutralize, or destroy other groups. Some cards have a second number representing *Transferable Power* (GP), which can assist attacks elsewhere.
*   **Resistance (R)**: The difficulty an attacker faces when attempting to capture or affect the group.
*   **Income (I)**: Megabucks generated at the start of each turn.
*   **Alignments**: Up to 10 alignments (Government, Corporate, Liberal, Conservative, Peaceful, Violent, Straight, Weird, Criminal, Fanatic) that grant compatibility modifiers (+4 for same, -4 for opposite).

Below is the complete database of the **83 classic group cards** (and their Deluxe upgrades), organized by their primary alignment categories, alongside our proposed mechanical translation into a modern digital card game experience.
