# Suggested New Card Ideas

This document catalogs an additional list of suggested cards for the modern adaptation of the Illuminati game, expanding upon our existing list with more modern internet culture, conspiracy theories, and real-world events.

## Conspiracy Theories & Paranoia
- **5G Microchips**: The widespread pandemic-era conspiracy theory that COVID-19 vaccines contained microscopic 5G tracking chips engineered by Bill Gates.
- **Wayfair Cabinets**: A viral QAnon conspiracy theory from 2020 claiming that over-priced industrial cabinets sold on Wayfair, which bore human names, were actually fronts for child trafficking.
- **Birds Aren't Real**: A satirical conspiracy theory that evolved into a massive movement, claiming the US government killed all birds and replaced them with surveillance drones.
- **15-Minute Cities**: An urban planning concept to make amenities accessible within a 15-minute walk, which sparked massive protests and conspiracy theories claiming it was a WEF plot for climate lockdowns.
- **Tartaria Mud Flood**: A sprawling, TikTok-fueled alternative history theory asserting that an advanced global empire called Tartaria was wiped out in the 19th century by a deliberate "mud flood," and classic architecture is actually inherited from them.
- **MedBeds**: A grift-heavy conspiracy theory promising the existence of suppressed alien or military "holographic medical beds" capable of instantly curing all diseases and reversing aging.
- **Frazzledrip**: One of the darkest QAnon legends, alleging the existence of a video found on Anthony Weiner's laptop showing Hillary Clinton and Huma Abedin committing a gruesome ritual sacrifice.
- **Chemtrail Pilots**: The classic conspiracy theory that commercial and military pilots are secretly spraying chemical or biological agents into the atmosphere to control weather or population health.
- **Denver Airport Gargoyles**: The genuine weirdness of the Denver International Airport—featuring apocalyptic murals, Masonic cornerstones, and a terrifying demon horse statue—fueling theories that it houses an underground bunker for the New World Order.
- **Vaxx Shedding**: The anti-vaccine claim that vaccinated individuals "shed" harmful spike proteins, leading to bizarre sub-conspiracies where anti-vaxxers fear catching the vaccine from proximity to others.
- **Havana Syndrome**: The mysterious cluster of neurological symptoms experienced by US diplomats, theorized to be caused by foreign microwave or sonic weapons, though official intelligence reports remain inconclusive.
- **Simulation Glitch**: The belief, popularized by internet videos of birds freezing mid-air or people lagging, that reality is a computer simulation that occasionally fails to render properly.

## Internet Culture & Brain Rot
- **TradWife Influencers**: The heavily-curated social media trend of women promoting submission to their husbands and aesthetic mid-century domesticity, often serving as a soft-launch for far-right ideologies.
- **Hawk Tuah Girl**: A viral street-interview meme that completely consumed the internet in 2024, representing the absurd speed at which average citizens can become global, monetized micro-celebrities.
- **Soyjaks**: The endlessly proliferating MS Paint-style meme faces used in internet culture wars to depict one's opponents as hysterical, unmasculine, or irrational.
- **Mewing**: A massive TikTok trend among teenage boys involving pressing the tongue to the roof of the mouth to allegedly change jawline structure, becoming a staple of "Looksmaxxing" culture.
- **Looksmaxxing**: A hyper-obsessive internet subculture focused on extreme aesthetic self-improvement, ranging from skin routines to "bone-smashing," often overlapping with incel ideology.
- **Brain Rot**: The collective term for the hyper-stimulating, algorithmically generated barrage of short-form content that critics argue is fundamentally altering the attention spans of the youth.
- **Harambe's Revenge**: The satirical (but deeply felt) internet folklore that the 2016 shooting of Harambe the gorilla in the Cincinnati Zoo fundamentally altered the timeline, plunging the world into chaos.

## Politics & Scandals
- **Four Seasons Total Landscaping**: The legendary 2020 press conference where Rudy Giuliani challenged the election results not at the luxury hotel, but in the parking lot of a landscaping business between a crematorium and a sex shop.
- **Paul Pelosi's Hammer**: The brutal 2022 politically motivated home invasion and attack on Nancy Pelosi's husband, which immediately spawned localized right-wing conspiracy theories and disinformation about the attacker's motives.
- **George Santos Volleyball**: A nod to the spectacular web of lies spun by disgraced Congressman George Santos, who falsely claimed, among many other things, to have been a star college volleyball player.
- **Taylor Swift Psyop**: The bizarre right-wing media panic in early 2024 alleging that pop superstar Taylor Swift and Travis Kelce's relationship was a deep-state psychological operation rigged to swing the Super Bowl and re-elect Joe Biden.
- **Fake Melania**: The persistent internet theory, fueled by uncanny photos and varying heights, that the Trump campaign frequently uses body doubles to stand in for Melania Trump at public events.
- **Gazpacho Police**: Representative Marjorie Taylor Greene's infamous malapropism where she accused Nancy Pelosi of employing the "gazpacho police" (instead of the Gestapo), highlighting the absurdity of modern political rhetoric.
- **Q Shaman**: Jacob Chansley, the face of the January 6th Capitol riot, who stormed the Senate floor wearing face paint, a fur headdress, and horns, becoming a permanent visual symbol of the insurrection.
- **Hunter's Art Gallery**: The ethical quagmire surrounding Hunter Biden selling his novice abstract artwork for half a million dollars to anonymous buyers while his father occupied the White House.
- **Corn Pop**: Joe Biden's infamous, rambling anecdote about confronting a razor-wielding gang leader named "Corn Pop" at a public pool in the 1960s, widely mocked for its bizarre delivery.
- **Pizzagate Basement**: The 2016 precursor to QAnon that led a man to fire an assault rifle inside the Comet Ping Pong pizzeria in D.C., demanding to see a non-existent basement where he believed a child sex ring was housed.
- **Sovereign Citizens**: A decentralized movement of pseudo-legal grifters who believe they are exempt from all US laws, taxes, and vehicle registration by uttering specific "magic words" to judges and police.
