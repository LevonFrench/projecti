# Card Counts and Roster Lists by Edition

This document details the card counts and rosters for Steve Jackson's classic *Illuminati* card game, its early Pocket Box expansions, modern Deluxe versions, Second Edition upgrades, and the *Illuminati: New World Order (INWO)* collectible card game.

---

## 1. Classic Boxed Board Game Chronology

Unlike collectible card games, the boxed board game editions use a fixed set of cards. Over successive releases, older cards were retired or renamed to keep the political and cultural satire current.

### 1.1 Original "Pocket Box" Base Game (1982)
*   **Total Count**: 54 cards
*   **Illuminati Cards (6)**: 
    *   Bavarian Illuminati, Bermuda Triangle, Discordian Society, Gnomes of Zurich, Servants of Cthulhu, UFOs.
*   **Group Cards (42)**: 
    *   Antiwar Activists, Big Media, Boy Sprouts, Cattle Mutilators, CFL-AIO, C.I.A., Cycle Gangs, Democrats, Eco-Guerrillas, Elders of Zion, F.B.I., Feminists, Fiendish Fluoridators, Fred Birch Society, Gun Lobby, Hollywood, International Cocaine Smugglers, International Communist Conspiracy, I.R.S., KKK, Local Police Departments, Madison Avenue, The Mafia, Moonies, Moral Minority, Multinational Oil Companies, Nephews of God, Parent/Teacher Agglomeration, The Phone Company, Post Office, Punk Rockers, Reformed Church of Satan, Republicans, Science Fiction Fans, Semiconscious Liberation Army, SMERSH, SMOF, Society for Creative Anarchism, South American Nazis, The United Nations, Wargamers.
*   **Special Cards (6)**: 
    *   Bribery, Computer Espionage, Deep Agent, Interference, Market Manipulation, Media Campaign.

### 1.2 Pocket Box Expansion Set 1 (1983)
*   **Total Count**: 28 cards (added to the base deck)
*   **New Illuminati (1)**: 
    *   Society of Assassins
*   **New Groups (23)**: 
    *   American Autoduel Association, Anti-Nuclear Activists, Clone Arrangers, Evil Geniuses for a Better Tomorrow, Fast Food Chains, Federal Reserve, Junk Mail, L-4 Society, Mini-minds, Nuclear Power Companies, Orbital Mind Control Lasers, P.T.A., Post Office, Postal Service, Professional Assassins, Russian Revolutionaries, Survivalists, Texas, Tobacco & Liquor Companies, Underground Newspapers, United Nations, Video Games (plus Blank Cards).
*   **New Specials (4)**: 
    *   Assassination, Murphy's Law, Secrets Man Was Not Meant To Know, plus 1 more.

### 1.3 Pocket Box Expansion Set 2 (1983)
*   **Total Count**: 28 cards
*   **New Illuminati (1)**: 
    *   The Network
*   **Curation/Rotations**: 
    *   *Fred Birch Society* replaced by **Congressional Wives**.
    *   *Elders of Zion* replaced by **TV Preachers**.
    *   *Wargamers* replaced by **Tabloids**.
*   **New Groups**: Includes Comic Books, Loan Sharks, and others to complete the 28-card count.

### 1.4 Pocket Box Expansion Set 3: Brainwash (1985)
*   **Total Count**: 28 cards
*   **Features**: Introduced the alignment-altering track, Propaganda rules, and the official **Media Group** modifier (+4 when controlling or destroying other Media cards).
*   **Roster**: Added cards specifically interacting with alignment shifting.

### 1.5 Deluxe Edition (1987 / 1999 / 2007)
*   **Total Count**: 110 cards
*   **Relationship**: Combines the original Pocket Box base game + Expansion 1 + Expansion 2. 
*   **Roster**: Contains all 8 Illuminati groups, 83 Group cards, and 19 Special cards. Dated pocket-box cards were officially retired or renamed to match this curated standard set.

### 1.6 Illuminati Second Edition (2018 Overhaul)
*   **Total Count**: 110 cards
*   **Relationship**: An upgrade of the Deluxe Edition. Swaps out retired political references for contemporary targets.
*   **Roster**:
    *   *Illuminati (8)*: Same 8 primary factions.
    *   *Groups (85)*: Swapped older groups for modern additions like **Bloggers**, **Flash Mobs**, **LulzSec**, etc.
    *   *Specials (17)*: Streamlined action deck.

## 2. Modern Deluxe Board Game Expansions

These expansions can be mixed into the *Deluxe Edition* or *Second Edition* decks.

### 2.1 Bavarian Fire Drill (2007)
*   **Total Count**: 110 cards
*   **Features**: Introduced **Artifacts** (objects attached to host groups that grant static buffs or abilities and can be stolen).
*   **Key Roster**: Includes Bloggers, Reality Shows, Intelligent Design, the Shroud of Turin, and the Holy Grail.

### 2.2 Mutual Assured Distraction (2010)
*   **Total Count**: 23 cards
*   **Features**: Introduced **New World Order (NWO)** cards which apply a global rule modifier to the entire board.
*   **Roster**: 18 NWO cards, 5 Special action cards.

### 2.3 Alternative Truths (2018)
*   **Total Count**: 125 cards (including 3 blanks)
*   **Features**: Introduced the new playable Illuminati group **Shangri-La** (peaceful/healing alignment focus) and additional NWO cards.

### 2.4 Illuminati 2020 (2020)
*   **Total Count**: 26 cards
*   **Features**: Pandemic-themed rules and cards.
*   **Key Roster**: #BLACKLIVESMATTER, Statue Smashers, Mask Designers.

## 3. CCG Spinoff: Illuminati: New World Order (INWO)

Unlike the board game, INWO is a collectible card game (CCG) utilizing starter decks and randomized booster packs.

*   **INWO Base Set (1994/1995)**: **412 cards** (409 core set + 3 promotional cards).
*   **INWO: Assassins Expansion (1995)**: **125 cards** (focusing on assassination plots and stealth targets).
*   **INWO: SubGenius Standalone/Expansion (1998)**: **100 cards** (Church of the SubGenius crossover).
*   **INWO: Bavarian Fire Drill Expansion (1998)**: **110 cards** (final print run containing Artifacts).
