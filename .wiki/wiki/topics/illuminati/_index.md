This section serves as a comprehensive mechanical and thematic reference for the classic card game **Illuminati** (first published in 1982 by Steve Jackson Games, with Deluxe and collectible variations). 

Our modern adaptation utilizes the strategic alignment principles, power hierarchies, and asymmetric goals of the original game, translating them into a modern digital card game experience.

## Core Rules & Mechanics Overview

In the original game, players draw and control groups to form a **Power Structure** tree:
1. **The Treasury (Megabucks)**: Megabucks (MB) are the primary resource. They are collected as income at the start of each turn and stored in individual group treasuries or the Illuminati treasury. Money can be spent to boost attacks (both control and destruction attempts) or bolster defenses.
2. **Control Tree**: An controlled Group card must be attached to an outward-pointing arrow on the card controlling it. Most groups have one inward arrow (top/left) and up to three outward arrows (top, right, bottom, left). If all arrows are occupied, no more groups can be controlled from that node.
3. **Alignments**: Factions and groups are defined by their alignments. Similar alignments aid in control attempts, while opposite alignments make it easier to destroy.
   - **Government** (opposes Corporate)
   - **Liberal** (opposes Conservative)
   - **Peaceful** (opposes Violent)
   - **Weird** (opposes Straight)
   - **Communist** (present in older sets, opposes Government/Capitalist in some versions, but generally acts as its own alignment)
   - **Criminal** (has no opposite)
   - **Fanatic** (opposes itself)

## Design Implications for a Modern Adaptation

When building a modern card game based on these mechanics:
- **Asymmetric Goals**: Keeping distinct faction-specific victory conditions encourages highly thematic playstyles.
- **Alignment Synergies**: Alignment systems provide a natural framework for deck building, synergistic effects, and strategic counter-play.
- **Treasuries as Energy**: The classic Megabucks treasury system can be adapted into localized energy pools, resource management, or custom card-level momentum tracks.
