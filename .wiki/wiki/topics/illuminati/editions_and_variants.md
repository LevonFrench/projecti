# Chronology of Illuminati Editions, Variants, and Spinoffs

Over its forty-year history, Steve Jackson Games has published numerous editions, expansions, and spinoffs of the *Illuminati* card game. This reference sheet details the chronology and components of each release, which guides our balancing decisions and aesthetic choices for the modern adaptation.

---

## 1. Boxed Board Game Editions

These editions utilize the classic board-game setup: a shared deck of Group and Special cards, paper or plastic Megabucks currency, and player boards or tracking sheets.

### 1.1 Original "Pocket Box" Edition (1982)
*   **Format**: Small plastic pocket box.
*   **Components**: A rulebook, a sheet of 54 cards (which players had to cut out themselves with scissors), sheet of paper Megabucks currency, and a single six-sided die.
*   **Notes**: Featured minimalist black-and-white line art. Despite its basic components, it gained a massive following and won the 1982 Origins Award for Best Boardgame.

### 1.2 Deluxe Edition (1987)
*   **Format**: Standard large board game box.
*   **Components**: Pre-cut color cards, card stands for the Illuminati cards, cardboard Megabucks counters, and a two-sided box cover.
*   **Expansion Integration**: Consolidated most of the cards and rule updates from the early *Pocket Box Expansion Sets 1–4*. It replaced several dated card concepts with newer topical groups (e.g., swapping the *Elders of Zion* for *TV Preachers* and *Fred Birch Society* for *Congressional Wives*).

### 1.3 Deluxe Edition (2007 Version)
*   **Format**: Large box, updated cover art.
*   **Components**: Redesigned rulebook and upgraded glossy card stock. This version served as the foundation for the newer Deluxe expansions like *Bavarian Fire Drill* (2007), *Mutual Assured Distraction* (2010), and *Alternative Truths* (2018).

### 1.4 Illuminati Second Edition (2018)
*   **Format**: Upgraded large box.
*   **Components**: Over 100 new cards, completely new color illustrations by artist **Lar deSouza**, heavy-duty cardboard tracking tokens, and a larger, modernized rulebook.
*   **Mechanics**: Updated various card rules to align with modern political and cultural contexts, while maintaining the exact core combat formulas.

### 1.5 Illuminati 2020 (2020)
*   **Format**: Specialized release/expansion focused on the specific cultural, geopolitical, and public health shifts of the year 2020.

## 2. Collectible Card Game (CCG) Spinoff

### 2.1 Illuminati: New World Order (INWO) (1994)
*   **Format**: Collectible card game starter decks and booster packs.
*   **Base Sets**:
    *   **Limited Edition (1994)**: Black borders. Extremely collectible.
    *   **Unlimited Edition (1995)**: Blue borders. Corrected text typos and refined rules.
    *   **Factory Set (1995)**: One of every card from the base set packaged in a collector's box.
*   **Expansions**:
    *   *Assassins* (1995) — 125 cards.
    *   *SubGenius* (1998) — 100 cards.
    *   *Bavarian Fire Drill* (1998) — 110 cards.
*   **Mechanical Shift**: Replaced the shared deck and action points with custom deck-building, local Action Tokens, and Plot/Event cards.

## 3. Standalone Games and Digital Variants

### 3.1 Illuminati: SubGenius (2001)
*   **Format**: Standalone card game.
*   **Theme**: Focused entirely on the parody religion of the **Church of the SubGenius**, Slack, and Bob Dobbs.
*   **Compatibility**: Designed as a standalone game for up to 4 players, but included rules allowing it to be mixed directly into the *Deluxe Illuminati* deck to expand the faction list.

### 3.2 Illuminati: Crime Lords (2004)
*   **Format**: Standalone board game.
*   **Concept**: Steve Jackson adapted the network control arrow mechanics of *Illuminati* to a street-level mobster theme.
*   **Gameplay**: Players control mob bosses rather than secret societies, attempting to control blocks, corrupt local police, run illegal rackets, and wipe out rival gangs. It replaced global alignments with regional turf control and criminal activities.

### 3.3 Steam Digital Version (2020)
*   **Format**: Digital video game on Steam, developed by RadioFree.Net.
*   **Features**: Single-player vs. AI mode, and cross-platform online multiplayer. It recreates the classic card-table experience, automating the math calculations for Attack rolls, treasury spends, and arrow alignments.
