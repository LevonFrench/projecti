# Special Cards Database

In the classic game of **Illuminati**, **Special cards** are powerful, one-time-use action cards that bend the standard rules of play. They can cancel attacks, manipulate dice rolls, siphon funds, or alter alignments.

Below is the mechanical breakdown of all **15 classic Special cards** and our design proposals for adapting them into a modern digital card game layout.

---

## 1. Secrets Man Was Not Meant to Know
*The ultimate cancel.*

### Classic Effect
*   Can be played at any time to immediately cancel any other Special card as it is played. The cancelled Special card is discarded with no effect.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Cabal Silence*
*   **Category**: **Instant Interrupt**
*   **Effect**: Playable out-of-turn. Immediately negates and discards any special action card played by an opponent.

---

## 2. Assassination
*Silent neutralization of an active asset.*

### Classic Effect
*   Provides a +10 bonus to any attempt to destroy a group, or immediately neutralizes a group if played during an attack.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Liquidation*
*   **Category**: **Removal Action**
*   **Effect**: Instantly destroys an active non-Illuminati card on the board, bypasses standard defense checks, and sends it to the discard pile.

---

## 3. Bribery
*Bypassing defenses with raw wealth.*

### Classic Effect
*   Allows automatic control of any Corporate or Criminal group without rolling, or adds +10 to the control attempt.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Hostile Takeover*
*   **Category**: **Capture Action**
*   **Effect**: Allows the player to capture or take control of a target Corporate or Criminal card on the board without needing to meet normal capture conditions or roll.

---

## 4. Computer Espionage
*Infiltrating information systems.*

### Classic Effect
*   Allows a player to examine another player's hand of Special cards, or steal money from a target treasury.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Data Breach*
*   **Category**: **Hand Disruption / Theft**
*   **Effect**: View the opponent's entire hand of cards and discard one card of your choice, or siphon up to 5 resource points from their reserve pool.

---

## 5. Deep Agent
*Double-cross and subversion.*

### Classic Effect
*   Played to immediately take control of any action declared by another player, redirection of target, or canceling the action.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Subversion*
*   **Category**: **Instant Redirect**
*   **Effect**: Immediately intercept and redirect a target action declared by another player, choosing a new target of your choice.

---

## 6. Interference (2 Cards in Deck)
*Disrupting coordination.*

### Classic Effect
*   Can be played during any attack to add a +4 bonus to the defense or subtract -4 from the attack.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Jamming Signal*
*   **Category**: **Defense Buff / Attack Debuff**
*   **Effect**: Played during a conflict phase. Grants a defensive buff to an allied card or applies an attack penalty to an attacking opponent card.

---

## 7. Market Manipulation
*Controlling the financial flow.*

### Classic Effect
*   Doubles the income of all Corporate groups controlled by the player for one turn, or causes a crash.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Market Spike*
*   **Category**: **Resource Multiplier**
*   **Effect**: Doubles all resource income generated by your Corporate cards on the next turn, or forces an opponent's Corporate nodes to yield zero resource income.

---

## 8. Media Campaign
*Shaping perception.*

### Classic Effect
*   Adds +5 (or subtracts -5) to any control or neutralization attempt by manipulating media alignments.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Alignment Modulation*
*   **Category**: **Utility Action**
*   **Effect**: Temporarily changes or shifts the alignments of a target card on the board for the duration of the turn to gain compatibility bonuses.

---

## 9. Murphy's Law
*If anything can go wrong, it will.*

### Classic Effect
*   Forces a player to reroll any dice roll that was just made, or causes a declared action to automatically fail.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *System Glitch*
*   **Category**: **Fail State Trigger**
*   **Effect**: Playable when the opponent triggers a random or high-value card effect. Forces them to re-evaluate the outcome or outright cancels the action's execution.

---

## 10. Senate Investigating Committee
*Official inquiries stalling operations.*

### Classic Effect
*   Prevents one player from using any of their Special cards, or spending any Megabucks from their Illuminati treasury, for one full turn.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Audit Lock*
*   **Category**: **Disrupt Action**
*   **Effect**: Prevents a chosen opponent from playing special action cards or spending resources from their central treasury pool on their next turn.

---

## 11. Slush Fund
*Hidden reserves.*

### Classic Effect
*   Allows the player to draw 15 Megabucks immediately from the bank and place it in their Illuminati treasury.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Off-the-Books Cash*
*   **Category**: **Resource Injection**
*   **Effect**: Instantly adds a massive burst of resource points to the player's central pool. This card is exiled from the game after one use.

---

## 12. Swiss Bank Account
*Fortified wealth.*

### Classic Effect
*   Allows a player to move up to 10 Megabucks to a secure "Swiss Bank Account" where it cannot be stolen or targeted by any Special cards.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Secured Vault*
*   **Category**: **Resource Shield**
*   **Effect**: Shields a portion of your resources, rendering them immune to siphoning, theft, or target actions by opponent cards.

---

## 13. Whispering Campaign
*Subversive rumors.*

### Classic Effect
*   Changes the alignment of any one group card for one turn, or lowers its resistance by 4.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Smear Campaign*
*   **Category**: **Defense Debuff**
*   **Effect**: Decreases the defense or resistance value of a target card by 4 for the duration of the turn.

---

## 14. White Collar Crime
*Embezzlement and financial schemes.*

### Classic Effect
*   Immediately steals 5 Megabucks from any other player's treasury and transfers it to your own.

### Modern Adaptation Concept
*   **Action Name**: *Embezzle*
*   **Category**: **Theft Action**
*   **Effect**: Instantly transfers a set amount of resource points from the target opponent's treasury directly to the player's treasury.
