# New Illuminati Card Ideas

This document catalogs a list of potential new cards for the modern adaptation of the Illuminati game, sourced from contemporary internet culture, conspiracy theories, and real-world events.

## Personalities & Celebrities
- **Rick Ross Beard**: Hip-hop mogul Rick Ross's famously perfectly manicured beard, which occasionally sparks online conspiracy theories that it's dyed, glued on, or synthetic.
- **Guy Had Sex With Female Bigfoot And Didn't Like It**: A viral sensation and cryptid community controversy stemming from a dog trainer's YouTube video claiming a female Sasquatch sexually assaulted him in the woods.
- **Lindsey Graham**: The notoriously malleable Republican Senator from South Carolina, known for his dramatic shifts in political loyalty and emotional cable news appearances.
- **Island Boys**: The heavily tattooed, viral TikTok twins known for their bizarre aesthetic and freestyle rapping in a pool, representing peak attention-economy internet celebrity.
- **Corey Feldman's Ascension Millennium**: A spectacularly weird 2013 music video and song by former child star Corey Feldman, widely mocked for its bizarre aesthetic and choreography.

## Scandals & Crimes
- **Crack & Hooters Waitresses**: The salacious leaked contents of Hunter Biden's laptop, used by political opponents to paint him as compromised by drug addiction and sex workers.
- **Throw Away Gun In Dumpster**: The 2018 incident where Hunter Biden's sister-in-law threw his .38 caliber revolver into a grocery store dumpster, leading to federal gun charges.
- **Bathroom Botox at Lionel Richie's**: A reference to the 2004 federal investigation where an unlicensed doctor performed illegal cosmetic injections out of the bathroom of Diane Richie, Lionel Richie's ex-wife.
- **Diddy Parties**: The infamous, star-studded "freak off" parties hosted by hip-hop mogul Sean "Diddy" Combs, which became the center of massive federal sex trafficking and racketeering indictments in 2024.
- **PPP Loan**: The massive, systemic fraud associated with the COVID-19 Paycheck Protection Program, where billions of dollars in forgiven government loans were scammed to buy luxury cars and real estate.

## Conspiracy Theories & Panics
- **Gay Frogs**: Infamous InfoWars host Alex Jones's 2015 rant that "chemicals in the water that turn the freaking frogs gay," based on distorted research about the herbicide atrazine.
- **Hawaii Space Lasers**: Following the devastating 2023 Lahaina wildfires, conspiracy theorists claimed the fires were intentionally ignited by Directed Energy Weapons (DEWs) to clear land for a smart city.
- **Jewish Space Lasers**: A phrase mocking Representative Marjorie Taylor Greene's 2018 suggestion that California wildfires were started by solar-beaming satellites funded by the Rothschild family.
- **Healing Ear Wound**: Following the July 2024 assassination attempt on Donald Trump, the incredibly rapid and scar-less healing of his ear wound spawned conspiracy theories that the shooting was staged.
- **Fox News Guest Rubber Mask Face**: A persistent QAnon-adjacent conspiracy theory claiming that various politicians, celebrities, or TV guests are deceased and have been replaced by body doubles wearing hyper-realistic silicone masks.
- **Trust the Plan**: The core mantra of the QAnon conspiracy theory, urging followers to remain patient and faithful that a secret military operation is working behind the scenes.
- **Adrenochrome Withdrawal**: A foundational QAnon lore piece claiming that the global elite harvest "adrenochrome" from the blood of terrified children and look sickly when their supply is cut off.
- **Alpha-Gal Syndrome**: A condition transmitted by the Lone Star tick that causes a severe allergy to red meat, frequently incorporated into conspiracy theories as a bio-weapon designed to force the public into eating bugs.
- **Furry Boyfriend**: A generalized culture war trope; conservative politicians frequently use "furries in schools using litter boxes" as a fabricated moral panic to attack LGBTQ+ inclusivity.
- **Tunnels Jews**: The bizarre January 2024 discovery of unauthorized, secret tunnels dug by a rogue faction of Chabad-Lubavitch Hasidic students under their world headquarters in Crown Heights, Brooklyn.
- **Castrados**: Castrati were historical male singers castrated before puberty to retain high voices; modern mentions often tie into bizarre deep-web conspiracy theories about the elite music industry.

## Culture War & Internet Memes
- **Tuck-Friendly Swimsuits**: Target's 2023 Pride Month collection featured "tuck-friendly" swimwear, sparking massive conservative boycotts and costing the company billions in market cap.
- **Dylan Whatever Bud Lite**: Transgender influencer Dylan Mulvaney's brief 2023 sponsored post for Bud Light, which triggered a massive, culturally defining conservative boycott that crippled the beer brand's sales.
- **Shane Gillis Saves Bud Lite**: In a desperate attempt to win back the "bro" demographic after the Mulvaney boycott, Anheuser-Busch sponsored comedian Shane Gillis.
- **Jerk Off Dance**: Donald Trump's signature campaign rally dance move to the Village People's "YMCA," widely mocked online for its resemblance to a double handjob motion.
- **Elon Nazi Salute**: Controversies surrounding Elon Musk's ownership of X (formerly Twitter), including his interaction with right-wing extremist accounts and accusations of allowing Nazi imagery on the platform.
- **BBL Smell**: A viral internet rumor and meme claiming that women who get Brazilian Butt Lifts (BBLs) suffer from chronic bad body odor due to the surgery or difficulty of hygiene during recovery.
- **Too Fat for the Uber**: Viral instances of extreme obesity clashing with the gig economy, often resulting in public freakouts or debates about accommodation versus vehicle weight limits.
- **Street Takeover**: The illegal and highly dangerous urban phenomenon where crowds of youth block major intersections to drift and perform donuts in cars.
- **Pineapple Kool-Aid**: A modern, internet-subculture spin on the phrase "drinking the Kool-Aid," referring to buying into niche cults or hyper-specific online ideologies.

## Disasters & Dystopia
- **DDR Shooter**: A reference to Adam Lanza, the Sandy Hook shooter, who was obsessed with the arcade game Dance Dance Revolution and played it for hours at a time.
- **AI Tupac Video Game**: The growing trend of resurrecting dead celebrities via artificial intelligence, highlighted when Drake used an AI Tupac Shakur voice for a diss track.
- **Data Centers**: The massive, energy-devouring infrastructure required to train and run modern AI models, leading to land grabs, strained power grids, and tech companies proposing their own nuclear reactors.
- **Brown Tap Water**: A symbol of failing American municipal infrastructure, famously highlighting the Flint, Michigan water crisis where cost-cutting led to dangerous lead contamination.
- **Tornado Alley Moves In**: Climate change researchers observing that traditional "Tornado Alley" in the Great Plains is shifting eastward into more densely populated areas of the Midwest and Southeast.
- **Strait of Hormuz**: The critical Middle Eastern maritime chokepoint between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman, where global oil supplies are constantly threatened by geopolitical posturing.
- **Fentanyl Lean**: The "zombie-like" posture associated with severe opioid addiction and the fentanyl epidemic, frequently filmed in urban centers and used in political attack ads.

- **Jim Bakker Food Buckets**: Disgraced televangelist Jim Bakker's lucrative grift of selling bulk "survival food" buckets to his apocalyptic, end-times prepping audience.
- **Joel Osteen Affirmation Cube**: Megachurch pastor Joel Osteen's heavily commercialized "Inspiration Cube," an electronic device that plays pre-recorded daily affirmations for a hefty retail price.
